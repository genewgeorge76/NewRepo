Sealcoating

Protect asphalt from weather, oxidation, and daily wear.

Sealcoating helps preserve the flexibility and finish of asphalt surfaces while giving driveways and parking lots a clean, renewed appearance. It is a smart maintenance step for surfaces exposed to sun, water, vehicle traffic, and seasonal temperature swings.

Ideal for:

  • Residential driveways that need a refreshed finish
  • Parking lots with fading, minor surface wear, or light oxidation
  • Properties pairing sealcoating with crack filling and routine maintenance
  • Owners looking to extend pavement life and improve appearance

FAQ

Common asphalt paving and sealcoating questions.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Charlottesville, Albemarle County, and surrounding Shenandoah Valley communities including Waynesboro, Staunton, and Harrisonburg.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Share your property details, surface condition, and project goals and we will follow up with next steps, pricing guidance, and scheduling availability.

How often should asphalt be sealcoated?

Most driveways and parking lots benefit from sealcoating every two to three years, depending on traffic, sun exposure, and surface condition.

Can you repair cracks and potholes instead of repaving?

Often, yes. Crack filling, patching, and resurfacing can restore many surfaces without a full reconstruction when the base is still in good condition.
